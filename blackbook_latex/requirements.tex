\section{Non-Functional Requirements of Folium}
Correctness and clarity were considered the most important aspects. The value provided to the investor should be based on calculations; explanations have to be followed, and AI-generated words that provide a number will not be accepted. All missing numbers are replaced by the default values and explained on the results page in the section titled “How this was worked out.”

Security and privacy depend on the sensitivity of the data. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt with cost 12; sessions use RS256 JSON Web Tokens (access tokens for 15 minutes and refresh tokens for 7 days, rotated), kept in httpOnly cookies. Mutating requests need a CSRF token; two-step secrets are encrypted with Fernet. Each table owned by a user is secured with row-level security in PostgreSQL; the application connects with a non-superuser role to enforce the policies. The audit table is immutable. Abuse is prevented via rate limiting: 5 logins per minute, 3 sign-ups and 3 password reset requests per hour, 10 analyses per minute.

The system should stay functional even in case of failures. Failure of price or market data does not make the page unusable since default values are applied and explained, and the failure of explanation goes back to the default text. In terms of performance, one simulation for 1,000 paths for 30 years takes about 0.1 seconds, and the analysis is done in the background, which makes the response fast. In terms of reproducibility, the decision stores the snapshot of the market and portfolio, and the dataset, training, and simulation use static random seeds. In terms of maintainability, all access to the research code is concentrated in one bridge module, and model versioning can be done using configuration switches.

\section{Feasibility Study of Folium}
Technical feasibility is guaranteed since all the modules use well-matured open-source libraries. XGBoost, scikit-learn, and SHAP are standard choices for tabular modeling; DoWhy handles identification and refutation; FastAPI and asyncpg handle long tasks asynchronously; and PostgreSQL has native support for row-level security. The heavy computations – more specifically, training the classifier in about 81 seconds and running the causal study in around 54 seconds – are done offline. Meanwhile, one prediction and one simulation per request are sufficiently fast to be completed as background tasks.

Operational feasibility is guaranteed since the investor provides the information that is already known to them (income, loans, holdings) once, and each further analysis requires only holdings, action, and optional sentence. Any missing information is accounted for using disclosed defaults rather than rejecting the user, and explanations are easily understandable and even provided in Hindi to avoid any technical terms. Economic feasibility is provided since all software is open-source and free of charge, the market data are obtained from free providers, and optional e-mail and Gemini services have a free tier and are off by default.

\section{Assumptions and Constraints of Folium}
Every one of them considers only one individual position. The horizons are restricted up to 30 years, but 20 years is considered if no horizon is indicated. Taxes according to the new system apply in fiscal year 2025-2026; mutual funds are considered as equity funds; international price without duty and GST is used for gold; and everything is shown in nominal rupees. The personal profiles are based on take-home pay; hence, whenever the simulator needs the income prior to taxes, then it uses the inverse of the income-tax formula. The model uses the assumption of long-term growth rates of 11\% for shares, 9\% for gold, 6.75\% for fixed deposits, and 3\% for savings; sales charge of 0.15\%; and a habit reduces by half the influence on which it operates.
